# Poultrix Development Notes

Preserve the existing minimalist, responsive UI and keep hardware-specific communication behind a service/data-adapter layer. The AI confirms the growth stage; the batch day decides *when* the AI checks and *which* stage it may confirm (day 1 = batch start date; Grower from day 11, Finisher from day 25; once a day at 10:00 Asia/Manila, only until the next stage is confirmed; an observe-only pre-check on day 10 and day 24 raises a "looks ready" alert but never changes the stage; forward only). All schedule values are constants in `raspberry-pi/stage_schedule.py`. The day alone never changes the stage. The only other way to change it is the owner's manual "Confirm real stage" on the maintenance site (any direction). See `SPEC-stage-schedule.md`.
